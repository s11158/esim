### Блок 3: What a chemical peel is

**H2:** What a chemical peel is

A chemical face peel is a controlled application of acids that dissolve the bonds between dead cells on the surface of the skin and signal the deeper layers to renew. The skin sheds what it no longer needs and rebuilds collagen in response. Depth is what separates one peel treatment from another: a superficial peel works within the epidermis, a medium depth peel reaches the upper dermis.

/ 01 - Superficial peel
Glycolic, mandelic and salicylic acids in low concentrations. Light freshening, minimal or no visible flaking, safe to repeat in a course. This is the workhorse of peeling in Dubai.

/ 02 - Medium depth peel
Higher concentrations, including TCA peel protocols, reach the upper dermis and work on deeper pigmentation and post-acne texture. Stronger effect, real recovery time, strict sun rules afterwards.

/ 03 - PRX-T33, a peel without peeling
A combination of TCA with hydrogen peroxide and kojic acid that stimulates the dermis without damaging the surface. No flaking, no social downtime, and the one protocol we can run comfortably through the Dubai summer.

### Блок 6: Depth matters more than the brand of the acid

**H2:** Depth matters more than the brand of the acid

The name on the bottle matters far less than four things the doctor checks before your first chemical peel treatment.

/ 01 - Depth of the peel
Superficial, medium or a no-peeling protocol like PRX-T33. Depth decides both the result and the risk; going deeper than the skin needs buys nothing and costs recovery time.

/ 02 - Your skin phototype
Darker phototypes respond well to peels but are more prone to post-inflammatory pigmentation. That is an argument for gentler acids, lower concentrations and a longer course, not for skipping the treatment.

/ 03 - Season and the Dubai sun
UV levels here stay high all year. Aggressive peels in the middle of summer are a real risk of pigmentation, which is why we lean towards superficial protocols and PRX-T33 and keep the stronger options for the cooler months.

/ 04 - Home care between sessions
A peel is half the work. Without daily SPF 50, gentle cleansing and the right actives at home, the tone you gained comes back within weeks.

### Блок 14: FAQ Honest answers (5)

**H2:** Honest answers

**Will my skin flake?**
It depends on the depth. After a superficial peel there may be light flaking for 2-4 days, after a medium depth peel more. PRX-T33 does not cause flaking at all, which is why it is the most requested option here.

**Can I have a peel in the summer in Dubai?**
Yes, but not any peel. In the hot months we work with superficial protocols and PRX-T33 and postpone stronger medium depth peels to the cooler season. Daily SPF 50 is required either way, indoors and out.

**How many sessions do I need?**
Usually 4-6 with 2-4 week intervals. One session gives a fresh look before an event; a lasting change in tone and texture comes from a course.

**Is a chemical peel suitable for darker skin?**
Yes, with the right choice of acid and concentration. Darker phototypes are more prone to post-inflammatory pigmentation, so we start gentler, go slower and never push the depth further than the skin needs.

**What should I avoid after a peel?**
For the first days: sun without SPF 50, sauna and hammam, the pool, scrubs, retinol and acids at home, and picking at flaking skin. The doctor gives you a short written list after the session.

### Блок 14: FAQ Отвечаем честно (5)

**H2:** Отвечаем честно

**Будет ли шелушение?**
Зависит от глубины. После поверхностного пилинга возможно лёгкое шелушение 2-4 дня, после срединного - заметнее. PRX-T33 не вызывает слущивания вообще, поэтому его выбирают чаще всего.

**Можно ли делать пилинг летом в Дубае?**
Да, но не любой. В жаркие месяцы мы работаем с поверхностными протоколами и PRX-T33, а более сильные срединные пилинги переносим на прохладный сезон. SPF 50 каждый день обязателен в любом случае, в том числе в помещении.

**Сколько сеансов нужно?**
Обычно 4-6 с интервалом 2-4 недели. Один сеанс даёт свежий вид перед событием; устойчивое изменение тона и текстуры даёт только курс.

**Подходит ли пилинг для смуглой кожи?**
Да, при правильном выборе кислоты и концентрации. Смуглая кожа более склонна к поствоспалительной пигментации, поэтому мы начинаем мягче, идём медленнее и не уходим глубже, чем нужно коже.

**Чего нельзя делать после пилинга?**
Первые дни: солнце без SPF 50, сауна и хаммам, бассейн, скрабы, ретинол и кислоты дома, а также сдирание шелушения. Врач выдаёт короткий список после сеанса.
