### Блок 3: What dermal fillers are

**H2:** What dermal fillers are

A dermal filler is a soft gel based on hyaluronic acid, a substance the skin already produces. Injected at the right depth, it adds volume exactly where the face has lost it and lifts or defines the contour. The gel gradually breaks down and is absorbed by the body, so the result is reversible and the treatment can be repeated.

/ 01 - Volume where it is missing
With age, cheeks, temples and the under eye area lose fat and bone support. The filler restores that support and the face looks rested again.

/ 02 - Contour and proportion
Jawline and chin fillers build a cleaner lower-face line and balance the profile, which is why they are the fastest-growing filler requests in Dubai.

/ 03 - Smoothing of folds
Nasolabial folds and marionette lines soften once the volume above them is restored and the crease itself is filled with a lighter gel.

### Блок 14: FAQ Honest answers (5)

**H2:** Honest answers

**Will it look natural?**
Yes, if the volume is right. The doctor works in half-millilitre steps and stops when the face looks rested, not when the syringe is empty. Overfilled faces come from too much product, not from fillers as such.

**Does it hurt?**
A topical anaesthetic is applied first and most gels contain lidocaine. Patients describe pressure rather than pain; the under eye and nose areas are done with a cannula, which is gentler.

**How long do dermal fillers last?**
From 6 to 18 months depending on the zone, the gel and your metabolism. Firmer contour gels in the jawline and chin tend to last longer than soft gels in mobile areas.

**Can fillers be removed?**
Hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with a special enzyme within a day or two if you want to adjust the result. This is one of the reasons we work only with HA fillers.

**Fillers or Botox - which do I need?**
They solve different problems. Botox relaxes the muscles behind expression lines; fillers restore volume and contour. Many patients combine them, and the doctor will tell you honestly at the consultation which one your face actually needs.
