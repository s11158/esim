### Блок 3: What lip fillers are

**H2:** What lip fillers are

A hyaluronic lip filler is a soft gel based on a substance the body already produces. Placed at the right depth in the lip body or along the vermilion border, it adds volume, defines the outline and holds water inside the tissue. The gel is gradually absorbed, so the result is reversible and the shape can be adjusted at the next session rather than being locked in.

/ 01 - Volume, not size for its own sake
The doctor adds millilitres in small steps and looks at the lip against the nose, chin and smile line, so lip augmentation stays in proportion with the face.

/ 02 - Contour and definition
A light gel along the border sharpens a blurred outline, gives the cupid's bow its shape again and stops lipstick from bleeding.

/ 03 - Hydration without volume
A very soft gel or a lip skinbooster can be used purely for moisture and texture if you do not want your lips any bigger.

### Блок 14: FAQ Honest answers (5)

**H2:** Honest answers

**Does it hurt?**
Honestly, lips are more sensitive than other zones, but the procedure is well tolerated. A topical anaesthetic is applied for 15 to 20 minutes first and the gel itself contains lidocaine, so the first injection is the sharpest moment and the rest feels like pressure. Patients typically describe it as uncomfortable rather than painful, and a cannula makes it gentler again.

**How long do lip fillers last?**
From 6 to 12 months. Lips move constantly when you talk, eat and smile, so hyaluronic acid breaks down faster there than in static zones. A faster metabolism, sport and smoking shorten it; the second session usually lasts longer than the first because part of the gel is still in place.

**Will it look natural?**
Yes, when the volume matches your anatomy. Most natural results come from 0.5 to 1 ml placed with attention to the border and the cupid's bow. The doctor will tell you at the consultation if what you are asking for will not sit well on your face, and will suggest what will.

**Can lip fillers be dissolved?**
Yes. A special enzyme dissolves hyaluronic acid within a day or two, and the lips return close to their previous state. We also dissolve and correct work done at other clinics, after examination.

**How do I care for my lips afterwards?**
Cold compresses on the first evening, no sport, sauna, alcohol or hot drinks for 24 hours, no lip massage or scrubs for a few days, and drink water. Avoid dental work and flights for about a week if you can, and use a plain balm as often as you like.
